MangoMan

Free AI on your own computer

Ask in plain words. MangoMan makes your resume, emails, website, social posts and more, and saves them on your computer. It uses free AI, so it costs nothing.

  1. Open PowerShell. Press the Windows key, type PowerShell, and press Enter.
  2. Paste this line and press Enter.
    [Net.ServicePointManager]::SecurityProtocol=3072; irm __SITE__/install.ps1 | iex
  3. MangoMan opens in your browser. Paste your free NVIDIA key, click Get ready, then Open the coding screen.

Next time: open MangoMan from the Start menu or your desktop. A black window opens with it; keep it open while you use MangoMan.

Prefer a normal download?

Double-click it in your Downloads. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because MangoMan is new: click More info, then Run anyway. The line above avoids this warning and adds MangoMan to your Start menu.

  1. Open Terminal. Press Cmd + Space, type Terminal, and press Enter.
  2. Paste this line and press Enter.
    curl -fsSL __SITE__/install.sh | sh
  3. MangoMan opens in your browser. Paste your free NVIDIA key, click Get ready, then Open the coding screen.

Next time: press Cmd + Space, type MangoMan, and press Enter. A Terminal window opens with it; keep it open while you use MangoMan.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11, macOS 11 or later, or Linux.
  • Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge, to make PDFs. Edge comes with Windows.
  • A free NVIDIA key. MangoMan shows you how to get one in two minutes.
  • About 200 MB of internet the first time. Wi-Fi is better than mobile data.
