This skill adapts methods from three projects under the MIT License, whose
notices follow. MangoMan's changes are released under the same license.

- superpowers (https://github.com/obra/superpowers): test-driven development,
  systematic debugging, verification before completion, writing plans.
- andrej-karpathy-skills (https://github.com/forrestchang/andrej-karpathy-skills):
  the four rules (think before coding, simplicity, surgical changes,
  goal-driven execution).
- everything-claude-code (https://github.com/affaan-m/everything-claude-code):
  thin vertical slices, the security review table, silent failures, the
  safety guard.
