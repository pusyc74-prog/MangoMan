# Sources

1. Dental implants, NHS (UK National Health Service). https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dental-implants/
   - Implants are fitted under local anaesthetic; the whole process usually takes several months
   - With good care, implants can last many years; the crown may need replacing after 10 to 15 years
2. Dental implant surgery, Mayo Clinic. https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/dental-implant-surgery/about/pac-20384622
   - The jawbone grows around the implant (osseointegration), which can take several months
   - A bone graft may be needed when the jawbone is too thin or soft
3. Tooth loss and replacement options, Indian Dental Association. https://www.ida.org.in
   - Options to replace a missing tooth: implant, bridge or removable denture
   - A bridge needs the teeth on either side to be filed down to hold it
4. Survival of dental implants: a systematic review, Journal of Dental Research (2012). https://journals.sagepub.com/home/jdr
   - Reported 10-year survival of implants above 90% in the studies reviewed
