# Siri Millets: facts

- We are a farmer-owned brand from Hyderabad, selling millets since 2019
- Our foxtail millet (korra in Telugu, thinai in Tamil, kangni in Hindi) is grown by 120 farmer families in Anantapur district
- Sold unpolished, in 1 kg packs for ₹140
- Our kitchen method: rinse twice, soak 4 to 6 hours, cook 1 cup millet with 2.5 cups water, simmer covered 15 minutes, rest 5 minutes
- For a pressure cooker: 1 cup millet with 2 cups water, 2 whistles
- Soaked millet cooks fluffier; unsoaked needs 3 cups water and turns softer
- Our customers' favourites: foxtail millet upma, khichdi, lemon rice and dosa (batter: 1 cup millet, 1/4 cup urad dal, ferment overnight)
- Store in an airtight jar for up to 6 months
