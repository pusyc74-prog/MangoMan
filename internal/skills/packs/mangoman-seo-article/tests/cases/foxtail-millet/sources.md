# Sources

1. Indian Food Composition Tables 2017, ICMR-National Institute of Nutrition. https://www.nin.res.in/ebooks/IFCT2017.pdf
   - Foxtail millet: about 8 g dietary fibre and 12 g protein per 100 g (raw grain)
   - Milled rice: about 2.8 g dietary fibre and 8 g protein per 100 g
2. International Year of Millets 2023, Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations. https://www.fao.org/millets-2023/en
   - The UN declared 2023 the International Year of Millets, proposed by India
   - Millets grow in dry areas with little water
3. Shree Anna: millets for nutrition, Press Information Bureau, Government of India. https://pib.gov.in
   - India is the largest producer of millets in the world
   - Millets are called Shree Anna by the Government of India
4. Smart Food: millets, ICRISAT (International Crops Research Institute for the Semi-Arid Tropics). https://www.icrisat.org
   - Cooked foxtail millet has a lower glycaemic index than polished white rice in the studies reviewed
