# Sources

1. PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, Ministry of New and Renewable Energy. https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in
   - Central subsidy for homes: ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW, ₹18,000 for the 3rd kW; most for a home is ₹78,000
   - Apply on the national portal: register, choose a registered vendor, install, then the DISCOM inspects and installs the net meter
   - The subsidy is paid into the home owner's bank account after the inspection, usually within 30 days
2. Scheme guidelines, PM Surya Ghar, Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (2024). https://mnre.gov.in
   - The scheme aims to put rooftop solar on 1 crore homes
   - Collateral-free loans for systems up to 3 kW are offered by public sector banks
3. Tamil Nadu Solar Energy Policy 2019, Tamil Nadu Energy Development Agency. https://teda.in
   - Net metering is allowed for homes in Tamil Nadu; extra units are credited against the bill
4. Rooftop solar, Press Information Bureau, Government of India (2024). https://pib.gov.in
   - Homes can get up to 300 units of free electricity a month under the scheme
