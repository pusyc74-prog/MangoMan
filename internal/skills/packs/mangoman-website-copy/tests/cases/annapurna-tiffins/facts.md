# Facts

- Annapurna Tiffins cooks home-style vegetarian tiffins in a kitchen in Baner, Pune.
- We deliver to Baner, Aundh, Balewadi and Pashan.
- A weekly plan of 6 lunches costs Rs 780.
- A monthly plan of 26 lunches costs Rs 3,200.
- Each tiffin has 4 rotis, one dry sabzi, one dal or curry, rice and a sweet on Sundays only.
- Lunch reaches the door between 12:00 and 1:00 pm.
- Orders for the next day close at 8 pm.
- We use cold-pressed groundnut oil and no ajinomoto.
- Savita Joshi started the kitchen in 2019 with 6 customers.
- Today we send out about 240 tiffins every day.
- Customers can pause a plan for free with 1 day notice.
- Menu changes every day and is posted on WhatsApp at 9 pm.
- Jain meals are available at the same price.
- Pay by UPI at the start of the plan.
- Order or ask questions on WhatsApp at 98765 43210.
