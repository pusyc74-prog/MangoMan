# Facts

- Sharma Physio Care is a physiotherapy clinic in Malviya Nagar, Jaipur.
- Dr Anita Sharma, MPT in orthopaedics, treats every patient herself.
- She has treated patients for 14 years.
- We treat back pain, neck pain, knee pain, shoulder pain and sports injuries.
- We also help patients recover after knee and hip surgery.
- The first visit takes 45 minutes and costs Rs 600.
- Later sessions take 30 minutes and cost Rs 500.
- Most treatment plans have 6 to 10 sessions, and Dr Sharma tells you the number after the first visit.
- The clinic is open Monday to Saturday from 9 am to 7 pm.
- Home visits are available within 8 km of the clinic for Rs 1,000 a session.
- Exercise sheets with pictures are given to every patient.
- Parking is free in front of the clinic.
- Book on 99290 45566, by call or WhatsApp.
