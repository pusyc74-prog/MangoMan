# Facts

- Gurukul Maths Academy teaches maths to students of classes 8, 9 and 10 in Nashik Road.
- Each batch has a maximum of 12 students.
- Classes run 5 days a week, Monday to Friday, for 90 minutes.
- Batches meet at 5 pm and 7 pm.
- The fee is Rs 2,500 a month for classes 8 and 9.
- The fee for class 10 is Rs 3,000 a month.
- The first demo class is free.
- There is a written test every 2 weeks, and parents get the marks on WhatsApp.
- Suresh Kulkarni teaches all batches and has taught maths for 18 years.
- He holds an M.Sc. in mathematics from Pune University.
- Students may attend 1 missed class again in another batch for free.
- The classroom is at Shop 4, Datta Mandir Road, Nashik Road.
- Syllabus covered: Maharashtra State Board and CBSE.
- Call 98220 11223 between 10 am and 8 pm to book the demo.
