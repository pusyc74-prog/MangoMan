## Facts you can use
- First outlet opened in Palasia in 2021. We now serve about 1,200 cups of chai a day there.
- New outlet: Plot 22, Scheme 54, Vijay Nagar, Indore. Opens 18 October 2026, 7 am to 11 pm.
- Opening weekend (18 and 19 October): free bun maska with every kulhad chai.
- Menu: kulhad chai ₹30, masala chai ₹25, bun maska ₹40, poha ₹45, sabudana khichdi ₹60.
- Seats 40 people, with a rooftop sitting area.
- Chai is brewed on a coal sigdi, served in clay kulhads made by potters from Dewas.
- Customer review (Google, Ritika Jain): "The only place in Indore where the chai tastes like my nani's."
- Orders on Zomato and Swiggy from the new outlet start 25 October 2026.
