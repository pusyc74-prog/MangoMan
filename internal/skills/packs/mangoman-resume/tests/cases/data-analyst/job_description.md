# Data Analyst, Growth at Paisaboost

Role: Data Analyst
Company: Paisaboost (digital lending app, 4 million users)
Location: Bengaluru (hybrid)
Experience: 2 to 4 years

## About the role
You will work with the growth and product teams to find what makes users take
and repay their first loan. You will own funnel and retention reporting, design
and read experiments, and turn questions from business teams into clear answers.

## What you will do
- Build and maintain growth dashboards and the weekly business review
- Run cohort analysis on acquisition channels and retention
- Design and analyse A/B testing for onboarding and repayment flows
- Write production-quality SQL and Python for analysis
- Partner with product, marketing and risk teams (strong stakeholder management)

## Key skills
SQL, Python, Power BI, Tableau, A/B testing, cohort analysis, statistics, Excel, dbt, stakeholder management, forecasting

## Good to have
Experience in fintech or lending. Familiarity with BigQuery.
