# Performance Marketing Manager at Vaidyam Ayurveda

Role: Performance Marketing Manager
Company: Vaidyam Ayurveda, D2C ayurvedic wellness brand, ₹180 crore revenue
Location: Mumbai (Andheri East), full time in office
Experience: 6 to 10 years in D2C or e-commerce marketing

## The role
Own paid acquisition across Meta and Google, lower our CAC and grow repeat
revenue. You will manage a ₹1.5 crore monthly budget, a team of 4 and our
agency partners, and report to the CMO.

## Responsibilities
- Plan and run campaigns on Meta Ads, Google Ads and Amazon Ads
- Own CAC, ROAS and contribution margin targets
- Build retention journeys with the CRM team (CleverTap)
- Work with the Shopify and product teams on conversion rate
- Lead and grow a team of performance marketers

## Key skills
performance marketing, Meta Ads, Google Ads, Amazon Ads, CAC, ROAS, retention, Shopify, conversion rate, CleverTap, team leadership, budgeting, D2C
