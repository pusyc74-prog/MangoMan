      Self Healing System

      MangoMan watches your app, finds what broke and why, and fixes it in a safe copy. Nothing changes on your live app until you say yes.

      1. 1. Watches

        Every 5 minutes it checks that your app is up, answers normally and answers in under 5 seconds.

      2. 2. Finds the cause

        When something fails, it shows what broke and the likely reason, from the error and, when it has your code, your latest changes.

      3. 3. Fixes it safely

        It writes the fix in a separate copy of your app, has a test written for it, and tests it there. If the test fails, it tries again.

      4. 4. Asks you first

        You approve here. It then goes live and is tested again. If that test fails, MangoMan takes the change out and publishes the last good version again.

      Why it helps

      • Problems are caught within 5 minutes, not when a customer complains.
      • You get the likely cause in plain words, not just "site down".
      • Fixes are tested before they reach your customers, and undone if they fail.
      • Your live app is never changed without your approval.
      • It costs nothing extra: it uses the same free AI as the rest of MangoMan.
