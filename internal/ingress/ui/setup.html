MangoMan

Free AI on your computer. Three steps, about five minutes.

1

Connect NVIDIA's free AI

Done

NVIDIA gives free AI models to anyone with a free account. No card needed. Get a key, then paste it here.

  1. Open build.nvidia.com and sign in, or make a free account.
  2. Click your profile at the top right, then Settings, then API keys.
  3. Create a key and copy it. It starts with nvapi- and is shown only once.

The key is checked with NVIDIA and stays on this computer. MangoMan never sees what you ask the AI.
